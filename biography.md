## Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/).
My research asks how distributed learning and computation can remain reliable, private, and efficient under imperfect communication. I use tools from ***information theory and coding*** to design and analyse ***robust, efficient, and secure distributed AI*** over wireless networks, spanning mechanism design, performance analysis, and fundamental limits.

I welcome discussions on related research topics and potential collaborations. Feel free to get in touch.
